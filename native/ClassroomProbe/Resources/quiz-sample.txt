B. a whale
C. a tiger
答案：B
解析：whale（鲸鱼）生活在海里。
标签：动物

类型：选择
难度：3

B. a desk
C. a grape
答案：A
标签：学习用品, 单词

类型：选择
难度：3
I see with my ___.
A. ears
B. eyes
C. mouth
答案：B
标签：身体, 单词

类型：阅读
难度：3
文章：Lily has a small garden.
She grows tomatoes and sunflowers.
Every morning she waters them before school.
问：What does Lily grow?
A. apples and roses
B. tomatoes and sunflowers
C. carrots and tulips
答案：B
问：When does she water the garden?
A. every morning
B. every night
C. on Sunday
答案：A
标签：阅读理解
